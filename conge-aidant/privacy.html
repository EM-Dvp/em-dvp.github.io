Privacy Policy – Congé Aidant

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Congé Aidant, published by EM-Dvp, helps users manage and track carer’s leave hours. No user account is required.

1. Stored data and its use

Leave dates, durations, entered comments, information used to track reference periods and the medical certificate, and settings, including the part-time leave entitlement, are stored in the application’s storage on the device. They are used to calculate balances, display history, manage reminders and produce backups and statements requested by the user.

This leave data is not sent to EM-Dvp. The application does not send its contents to the AdMob advertising service. Android system backups and exports are described below.

2. Medical certificate

The application does not request or store a copy of the medical certificate. It only records tracking information, such as its submission date. There is no need to enter diagnoses or medical details in comments.

3. Exports, backups and transfers

Users can export and import leave records and the settings included in CSV backups, and generate PDF statements. Users choose where to save files and whether to share them. EM-Dvp does not automatically receive these files. Files saved or shared through a third-party service are subject to that service’s privacy practices.

The application’s configuration also allows Android system backups. Depending on device settings and the services provided by the device’s supplier, data and settings may be backed up, including through Google’s backup service, and later restored or transferred to another device. These operations depend on the system and do not take place on an EM-Dvp server.

4. Advertising – Google AdMob

A Google AdMob banner may appear on the home screen. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares technical data for ad delivery, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on configuration, privacy choices and the features used, this data includes:

  • IP addresses, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • device or advertising identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with the application and ads, such as launches, clicks or views;
  • diagnostic and performance information about the application and SDK.

Ad personalisation depends on consent choices and applicable rules. Non-personalised or limited ads may also involve processing some technical data. Data transmitted by the SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

For Google’s processing, retention practices and data rights, see Google’s Privacy Policy and information about Google’s responsibility for business data.

5. Consent and advertising choices

The application uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a form presents the available choices regarding advertising data processing.

When Google indicates that access to privacy options is required, the application displays an ad privacy entry in Settings. It allows users to reopen the form and change the available choices. Advertising ID controls are also available in Android settings, depending on the system version.

6. Data retention and deletion

Leave data and settings remain on the device until deleted. Leave records can be deleted from history, subject to reference-period consistency checks. To delete all local application storage, use Android settings: Apps → Congé Aidant → Storage → Clear data or Clear storage; labels vary by device. Uninstalling the application normally removes its local storage.

Previously exported CSV or PDF files are not deleted by these actions. Delete them separately, including any copies shared elsewhere. System backups are managed separately through the backup provider’s settings and may allow data to be restored after reinstallation. Deleting local data does not delete data already processed by Google, which is governed by Google’s own policies and privacy tools.

7. Data received by EM-Dvp and contact

EM-Dvp operates no server for storing leave records and cannot access or remotely delete locally stored data. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. Correspondence is used to handle your request and retained for as long as needed to follow it up, subject to applicable legal obligations.

For questions or requests concerning data actually held by EM-Dvp, contact EM-Dvp at contact.em.dvp@gmail.com. Depending on your circumstances and applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion and exercise other applicable rights. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application, services used or regulatory obligations. The update date appears at the top of this page.
