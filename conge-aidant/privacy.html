Privacy Policy – Congé Aidant

Last updated: August 30, 2026

The Congé Aidant application, published by EM-Dvp, has been designed to allow users to manage and keep track of their carer's leave hours. The protection of users' privacy is taken into account in the operation of the application.

1. Data stored by the application

Information entered into Congé Aidant, such as leave dates, duration, associated comments and information required to track reference periods, is stored locally on the user's device.

This data is not transmitted to EM-Dvp, and no user account is required to use the application.

2. Medical certificate

Congé Aidant does not request or store a copy of the medical certificate. The application only allows users to record the information necessary to keep track of it, such as its submission date.

3. Data backup and export

The application allows users to export and import their data for backup or transfer purposes. These operations are performed at the user's initiative.

EM-Dvp does not receive the files created in this way.

4. Advertising – Google AdMob

Congé Aidant uses the Google AdMob advertising service provided by Google. This service may process certain information relating to the device and the use of advertising, including for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where the user has given consent, ad personalisation.

Google's collection and processing of this information is subject to Google's privacy rules and policies.

For more information about how Google processes data in connection with its services, please see: Google's responsibility for business data .

5. Advertising consent

Where required by applicable regulations, particularly for users located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent mechanism may be presented regarding the use of data for advertising purposes.

Users may accept, refuse or manage their choices in accordance with the options provided.

6. Personal data transmitted to EM-Dvp

EM-Dvp does not directly collect any personal data entered into the Congé Aidant application and does not operate any server intended to store users' leave data.

7. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application, the services used or applicable regulatory requirements.
